# TrustinJr messaging guidelines

Version 1.2 · October 13, 2026 · Approved direction: mission-led website redesign.

## The center

**Mission:** To open a world of knowledge and discovery to every child, while keeping parents at the center of their learning journey.

**Conviction:** The future of learning shouldn’t come at the expense of childhood.

**Positioning:** Parent-guided AI discovery for early childhood, initially ages 4–5.

The opportunity comes first: little questions can become useful ideas, stories, counting challenges and things to notice in the world. Then demonstrate how the parent participates and what commitments govern the environment. Do not begin with fear of technology or guilt about screen time.

## Message hierarchy

| Layer | Approved expression | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Emotional headline | For every little why. | Preserve the original brand |
| Supporting promise | A world of wonder. Guided by you. | Pair possibility and parental agency |
| Category | Parent-guided AI discovery · Ages 4–5 | Five-second comprehension |
| Product | Questions, prepared discoveries, stories and early math; audio-first on Apple Watch, Parent Dock on iPhone | Explain what the parent is evaluating |
| Stage | In development & testing · Scripted preview | Set accurate expectations |
| Discovery promise | A world of discovery for children | Curiosity, small steps, thought, understanding, real-world connection |
| Parent promise | Meaningful guidance for parents | Participation, useful choices, appropriate visibility |
| Childhood promise | A childhood that isn’t for sale | No child advertising, sponsored answers or purchase pressure; finite experiences |

“Every child” describes our ambition. It does not mean universal access, all-age suitability, free pricing or confirmed compatibility. “Guided by you” includes a parent’s questions and shared exploration, not just restriction.

## Claims must have a status

| Say | Avoid |
|---|---|
| “Our commitment is no advertising to children.” | Privacy/compliance certification or guaranteed safety |
| “The sample preference changes this browser preview.” | Claiming the Moon switch is an exact native feature |
| “Family choices add to mandatory safeguards.” | Suggesting a parent can disable essential protections |
| “Designed to support understanding.” | Proven learning gains, accelerated development, mastery from taps |
| “The beta includes activity reports for some experiences.” | See everything your child learns; complete monitoring |
| “The website demo has no microphone capture or real child profile.” | We collect no data; every part of the native app is local |
| “Prepared discoveries and original recorded narration.” | Expert-reviewed lessons or live AI in the demo |
| “Audio-first.” | Screen-free, device-free or replacement for adult participation |
| “Family Setup onboarding remains unresolved.” | Universal Apple Watch compatibility |

Trustin is a recognizable learning character in a computer tool. Avoid AI-friend, emotional companion, babysitter, restricted-ChatGPT and replacement-teacher/parent framing. Never imply the character needs a child, is lonely, keeps secrets or feels disappointed when they leave.

No fabricated waitlist, App Store download, price, trial, scarcity, testimonials, award, certification or guaranteed launch. The email address still needs a delivery test before public launch.

## Editorial discipline

Use plain verbs: ask, notice, think, try, listen, count, explore, together. Explain scaffolding with an actual example. Keep evidence populations visible: studies of adolescents or broad 0–8 parent reports do not establish preschool AI benefits. Never turn a framework into an endorsement.

Keep one clear demo disclosure, local labels on illustrative controls, and deeper details in privacy/availability. Useful clarity should not be buried, but repeated warnings should not become the story. Preserve recorded scripts and their matching assets when refining surrounding marketing copy.

When product state changes, update availability first, then the bible, homepage and relevant metadata. Verify operational claims before publishing them.
