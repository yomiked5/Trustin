## Presentation contract

Keep product content inside the device. Keep descriptions, reasons and preview boundaries outside. Watch and phone retain independent fixed slots; longer rationale scrolls within its own panel. An outside swipe selects a feature. Inside-device touches remain in the product sample. Keep explicit arrows, selectors and skip links. No new autoplay, live API, analytics SDK, browser storage, backend or dependency.
